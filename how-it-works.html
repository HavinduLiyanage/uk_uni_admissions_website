THE PROCESS

How It Works

A structured, expert-led journey from your first question to your first day on a UK campus — completely free for Sri Lankan students.

01
Start here — no account needed

Discovery

"You have a goal — study in the UK"

You arrive with either a specific university in mind, a subject interest, a scholarship target, or simply a desire to study in the UK. No commitment needed at this stage.

02
Takes 10 minutes

Browse & Shortlist

"Find your perfect programme"

Browse 29+ universities and 500+ programmes filtered for Sri Lankan students. Filter by subject, degree level, city, and scholarship availability.

03
2 minute form

Submit an Enquiry

"Tell us what you are looking for"

Fill in a simple form — general or programme-specific. No lengthy application. Just your name, contact, and area of interest. Submitted directly to a dedicated counsellor.

04
Response within 24 hours

Counsellor Review

"Your dedicated counsellor reviews your profile"

A UK specialist counsellor reviews your enquiry, assesses your qualifications against programme requirements, and prepares a personalised shortlist and guidance plan.

05
We carry the load

Guided Application Preparation

"We prepare everything — you just review and sign"

Your counsellor handles the full application:

  • check_circle Document checklist and collection
  • check_circle Transcript formatting for UK requirements
  • check_circle Personal statement / SOP writing guidance
  • check_circle Reference letter coaching
  • check_circle Application form completion
  • check_circle Pre-submission quality review
06
You will never wonder what is happening

Submission & Tracking

"Application submitted — we track it for you"

Your application is submitted directly to the university. Your counsellor maintains contact with the admissions team and keeps you updated at every stage. If the university requests additional information, we handle it.

07
We stay with you until you board the plane

Outcome & Next Steps

"Offer received — what happens next"

Conditional or unconditional offer received. Your counsellor guides you through:

  • check_circle Accepting the offer
  • check_circle Meeting any conditions (IELTS, grades)
  • check_circle CAS letter collection
  • check_circle UK Student visa application
  • check_circle Pre-departure briefing
